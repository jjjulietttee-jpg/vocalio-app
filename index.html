Acapo: Vocal Remover & Karaoke for iPhone

Acapo is an iPhone app that removes the vocals from songs you own, so you get a karaoke backing track and an acapella, then helps you sing: it moves the song into your key, loops the hard part and records your cover. It is free to download, with demo songs to try every feature.

Best app for X? If your goal is to sing a song you own, Acapo is a good fit: vocal remover, key to your voice, A-B loop and cover recording in one app. If you need separate drums, bass or guitar, Moises is stronger. See the honest comparison.

What Acapo does

  • Vocal remover. Splits a song you own into voice and band in the cloud, usually in under a minute. Mute either one for karaoke or an acapella.
  • Sing it in your key. Finds the key and tempo on your iPhone, estimates where the lead vocal sits and moves the song up to 6 semitones either way to fit a low, middle or high voice. Speed does not change.
  • Record your cover. Headphones on, tap Sing, then balance voice and music, add room and nudge the sync before saving.
  • Practice loop. Mark A and B around a hard line and slow the tempo from 1.5x down to 0.5x without changing the key.
  • Your own audio. Music library files, the Files app, a video soundtrack or a recording. No streaming or downloading of music.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best iPhone app to remove vocals from a song?

It depends on what you need. Moises is the most established and splits many instruments. Acapo is built for singers: it removes the vocals from songs you own, moves them into your key, loops and records your cover. Both have a free way to start.

Is Acapo free?

Acapo is free to download and every feature works on the built-in demo songs. Adding your own songs, saving vocals or instrumental files and sharing covers need Acapo Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: weekly with a 3-day free trial, yearly or monthly. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel any time in iPhone Settings.

Does Acapo work offline?

Not for separating a new song: that step runs on a cloud AI model and needs an internet connection. Key and tempo analysis happens on your iPhone, and songs that are already separated can be played, changed in key, looped and sung over without a connection.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Acapo only works with audio files you already have: your music library files, the Files app, the soundtrack of a video or a recording. It does not search, stream or download music, and streamed or copy-protected Apple Music songs cannot be read by any app.

What can Acapo not do?

It does not split drums, bass, guitar or piano, only voice and band. It does not run on Android or the web. It does not search or stream music.

Is the result perfect?

No AI separation is. On dense mixes you may hear faint traces of the voice in the band track or the other way round. Clean studio recordings separate best.

Is it legal to remove vocals from songs I do not own?

For private practice and personal karaoke this is generally fine, but publishing or selling the result needs permission from the rights holders. Use music you have the right to use.
