Acapo vs Moises: a Cheaper Alternative for Singers on iPhone?

Updated October 2026 · By the Acapo developer

Moises is the bigger, more established app with more instrument stems; Acapo is a narrower tool for singers (voice or band, key to your voice, loop, record your cover). Whether Acapo is cheaper depends on how you pay, so compare current prices before you choose.

Quick answer. Pick Moises if you want separate drums, bass, guitar and piano or use more than an iPhone. Pick Acapo if you mainly want to sing: it splits a song into voice and band, moves it into your vocal range, loops and records your cover. Both have free ways to start.

Step by step

  1. Decide what you need to splitIf you need separate drums, bass, guitar or piano, choose Moises. If voice versus band is enough, Acapo covers it.
  2. Compare the free startMoises has a free tier with a limited number of uploads each month. Acapo is free to download with demo songs to try every feature; your own songs need Pro with a 3-day free trial on the weekly plan.
  3. Compare current pricesOn the US App Store listing on 2026-10-09, Moises Premium was $5.99/month or $39.99/year. Acapo's weekly plan was $4.99 with a 3-day trial, and its yearly plan $39.99. Check the live store pages, because both change.
  4. Try the singing toolsAcapo's Fit my voice, A-B loop and Sing recording are the reasons to choose it. Test them on the demo songs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Acapo cheaper than Moises?

Not necessarily. A weekly plan can cost more over a month than a monthly one, and the yearly prices we saw were similar. Compare the live store listings for your country.

Which has more stems?

Moises. It splits drums, bass, guitar, piano and more. Acapo splits only voice and band.

Which is better for singers?

Acapo is built around singing: it estimates where the lead vocal sits and shifts the song into your range, then records your cover. Moises also plays songs in any key and speed and has a very large user base.

Which is more established?

Moises: about 28,000 App Store ratings at 4.7 stars versus Acapo's new listing with no rating yet.

Do both work offline?

Both split songs in the cloud. Acapo plays already-split songs offline.

Does Acapo run on Android or the web?

No. Acapo is iPhone only. Moises is also on Android, web and desktop.

Is Acapo free?

Acapo is free to download and every feature works on the built-in demo songs. Adding your own songs, saving vocals or instrumental files and sharing covers need Acapo Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: weekly with a 3-day free trial, yearly or monthly. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel any time in iPhone Settings.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Acapo only works with audio files you already have: your music library files, the Files app, the soundtrack of a video or a recording. It does not search, stream or download music, and streamed or copy-protected Apple Music songs cannot be read by any app.
