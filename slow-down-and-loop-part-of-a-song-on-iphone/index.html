How to Slow Down and Loop Part of a Song to Practice on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Acapo developer

Use a practice player with an A-B loop and a tempo control that keeps pitch. In Acapo, open the Practice tab, tap A at the start of the hard part and B at its end, then slow the tempo until it feels easy.

Quick answer. In Acapo, open a song, go to Practice, tap A where the tricky part starts and B where it ends. The section loops. Slow the tempo down (from 0.5x up to 1.5x) without changing the key, and bring it back up as you improve.

Step by step

  1. Add a songTap Add a song and pick the source: your music library, the Files app, a video (Acapo takes its soundtrack) or a microphone recording. Acapo asks once for your consent to send the audio to its cloud separation service.
  2. Wait for the splitAcapo separates the song in the cloud into two tracks, the voice and the band (instrumental). It usually takes under a minute, depending on song length and connection.
  3. Open PracticeOpen the song in the studio and switch to the Practice tab.
  4. Mark A and BPlay to the start of the hard part and tap A, then tap B at its end. Acapo loops the section and highlights it on the waveform.
  5. Slow it downLower the tempo until you can play or sing the part cleanly. The key stays where it is.
  6. Bring it back upRaise the tempo in steps, and mute the voice if you want to sing the line yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Does slowing down change the pitch?

No. Tempo and key are separate controls in Acapo.

How slow can I go?

Tempo runs from 0.5x to 1.5x of the original speed.

Can I loop any part of a song?

Yes. Tap A and B on the Practice tab; the loop is highlighted on the waveform and you can switch it off or clear it.

Can I mute the singer while I practice?

Yes. Once the song is split, turn the voice down to practice with only the band, or turn the band down to study the vocal.

Is it good for instruments, not only singing?

The loop and tempo controls work for any song. Acapo only splits voice from band, so if you want to isolate a guitar or drums, a multi-stem app such as Moises is the better fit.

Is practice mode free?

You can try it on the built-in demo songs for free. Using your own songs needs Acapo Pro.

Does Acapo work offline?

Not for separating a new song: that step runs on a cloud AI model and needs an internet connection. Key and tempo analysis happens on your iPhone, and songs that are already separated can be played, changed in key, looped and sung over without a connection.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Acapo only works with audio files you already have: your music library files, the Files app, the soundtrack of a video or a recording. It does not search, stream or download music, and streamed or copy-protected Apple Music songs cannot be read by any app.
