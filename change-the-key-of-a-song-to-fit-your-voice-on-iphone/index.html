How to Change the Key of a Song to Fit Your Voice on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Acapo developer

Add the song to a key changer that works on the whole recording, set your voice as low, middle or high, and shift the pitch in semitones until the highest and lowest notes feel comfortable. Acapo does the first step for you with one tap.

Quick answer. In Acapo, add the song, open the Key tab and tap Fit my voice. The app detects the song's key and tempo on your iPhone, estimates where the lead vocal sits and moves the song by up to 6 semitones either way so it lands in your range. Speed stays the same.

Step by step

  1. Add a songTap Add a song and pick the source: your music library, the Files app, a video (Acapo takes its soundtrack) or a microphone recording. Acapo asks once for your consent to send the audio to its cloud separation service.
  2. Wait for the splitAcapo separates the song in the cloud into two tracks, the voice and the band (instrumental). It usually takes under a minute, depending on song length and connection.
  3. Tell Acapo your voiceChoose low, middle or high voice in onboarding or in Settings. This is how Acapo knows what range to aim for.
  4. Tap Fit my voiceIn the Key tab Acapo reads the song's key and where the lead vocal sits, then proposes a shift. You can also move the key yourself in semitone steps, up to 6 either way.
  5. Check and singListen to the band in the new key. Nudge it up or down if you want it easier or brighter, then sing along or record a cover.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which key suits my voice?

Tell Acapo whether your voice is low, middle or high. It estimates the range of the lead singer in the song and picks a shift that puts that line closest to your range. It is an estimate, so listen and adjust by ear.

Does changing the key change the speed?

No. Key and tempo are separate controls in Acapo. Tempo can be changed from 0.5x to 1.5x on its own.

How far can I shift a song?

Up to 6 semitones up or down in the key control.

Does Acapo show the original key?

Yes. It analyses the song on your iPhone and shows the detected key and tempo, and the key after your shift. Automatic key detection is an estimate and can be wrong on unusual songs.

Does it change the whole song or only the band?

The voice and the band are separate tracks. The key control moves the song as you play it, so you can sing over the shifted band.

Is key changing free?

You can try it on the built-in demo songs for free. Using it on your own songs needs Acapo Pro, because adding your own songs does.

Does Acapo work offline?

Not for separating a new song: that step runs on a cloud AI model and needs an internet connection. Key and tempo analysis happens on your iPhone, and songs that are already separated can be played, changed in key, looped and sung over without a connection.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Acapo only works with audio files you already have: your music library files, the Files app, the soundtrack of a video or a recording. It does not search, stream or download music, and streamed or copy-protected Apple Music songs cannot be read by any app.

Is the result perfect?

No AI separation is. On dense mixes you may hear faint traces of the voice in the band track or the other way round. Clean studio recordings separate best.
