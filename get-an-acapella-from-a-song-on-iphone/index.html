How to Get an Acapella From a Song on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Acapo developer

Use an AI vocal isolator: add the song, wait for the split, then keep only the voice track and save it. Acapo gives you the voice and the band as two separate tracks, and you can save either one.

Quick answer. In Acapo, add the song, wait about a minute for the split, then tap the Acapella preset in the Mix tab (band off, voice full). Save the vocals as its own file from the export menu, which needs Acapo Pro. Expect small traces of the music on dense mixes.

Step by step

  1. Add a songTap Add a song and pick the source: your music library, the Files app, a video (Acapo takes its soundtrack) or a microphone recording. Acapo asks once for your consent to send the audio to its cloud separation service.
  2. Wait for the splitAcapo separates the song in the cloud into two tracks, the voice and the band (instrumental). It usually takes under a minute, depending on song length and connection.
  3. Switch to the acapellaIn the Mix tab, tap the Acapella preset (band off, voice full). That is your acapella.
  4. Save the vocals (Pro)Open the export menu and choose Save vocals. You get the isolated voice as an audio file; Save instrumental gives you the band.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an acapella for free?

You can try separation on the built-in demo songs for free. For your own songs and for saving the vocals as a file you need Acapo Pro.

How clean is the acapella?

Good on most songs, never perfect. Dense mixes, heavy reverb and backing vocals can leave faint traces of the music or cut parts of the voice. Clean studio recordings separate best.

Does it separate backing vocals?

Acapo splits a song into voice and band. Backing vocals may land in either track depending on the song; it does not split lead from backing vocals.

Can I also get the instrumental?

Yes, the same split gives you both tracks, and you can save either.

What can I do with an acapella?

Practice singing along, study a vocal line, make a private cover or learn harmonies. Publishing or selling remixes needs permission from the rights holders.

Which formats can I save?

The app saves the tracks as audio files you can share to Files or other apps; it does not offer a choice of lossless formats.

Does Acapo work offline?

Not for separating a new song: that step runs on a cloud AI model and needs an internet connection. Key and tempo analysis happens on your iPhone, and songs that are already separated can be played, changed in key, looped and sung over without a connection.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Acapo only works with audio files you already have: your music library files, the Files app, the soundtrack of a video or a recording. It does not search, stream or download music, and streamed or copy-protected Apple Music songs cannot be read by any app.

Is it legal to remove vocals from songs I do not own?

For private practice and personal karaoke this is generally fine, but publishing or selling the result needs permission from the rights holders. Use music you have the right to use.
