How to Make a Karaoke Track and Record Yourself Singing on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Acapo developer

Remove the original vocals with an AI vocal remover, then record yourself over the instrumental in the same app. In Acapo, open the song, switch to Sing, put on headphones and tap Start singing.

Quick answer. Add a song to Acapo, wait for the split, open Sing, put on headphones and record. Acapo plays the band in your chosen key and records your voice, then lets you balance voice and music, add a little room and nudge the sync before you save the cover.

Step by step

  1. Add a songTap Add a song and pick the source: your music library, the Files app, a video (Acapo takes its soundtrack) or a microphone recording. Acapo asks once for your consent to send the audio to its cloud separation service.
  2. Wait for the splitAcapo separates the song in the cloud into two tracks, the voice and the band (instrumental). It usually takes under a minute, depending on song length and connection.
  3. Set the keyTap Fit my voice in the Key tab so the band sits in your range.
  4. Open Sing and put on headphonesWithout headphones the band leaks into the microphone and your cover sounds doubled. Acapo warns you if none are connected.
  5. RecordTap Start singing. After a short count-in the band plays and your microphone records.
  6. Mix and saveBalance your voice against the music, add some room, nudge the timing if your take feels early or late, then tap Save cover. Sharing the cover needs Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need headphones?

Yes, wired or Bluetooth. Without them the band leaks into the microphone and the cover sounds doubled.

What can I adjust after recording?

The balance between your voice and the band, a room (reverb) amount and a timing nudge of up to 150 milliseconds either way, to line your voice up with the music.

What format is the saved cover?

The cover is mixed and saved as an AAC audio file (.m4a) inside the app, from where you can share it if you have Pro.

Can I record over a song in a different key?

Yes. Set the key first; the band plays in the shifted key while you record.

Is recording free?

You can try the whole flow on the built-in demo songs. Songs of your own and sharing a finished cover need Acapo Pro.

Can I get just the karaoke track as a file?

Yes. With Pro, use the export menu to save the instrumental as its own file.

Does Acapo work offline?

Not for separating a new song: that step runs on a cloud AI model and needs an internet connection. Key and tempo analysis happens on your iPhone, and songs that are already separated can be played, changed in key, looped and sung over without a connection.

Can I use Apple Music or Spotify songs?

No. Acapo only works with audio files you already have: your music library files, the Files app, the soundtrack of a video or a recording. It does not search, stream or download music, and streamed or copy-protected Apple Music songs cannot be read by any app.

Is it legal to remove vocals from songs I do not own?

For private practice and personal karaoke this is generally fine, but publishing or selling the result needs permission from the rights holders. Use music you have the right to use.
