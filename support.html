How do I take the vocals out of a song?

On the main screen tap Add a song, pick where it comes from (music library, Files, a video or a recording) and Acapo separates it in the cloud, usually in under a minute.

How do I move a song to my key?

Open the song, switch to Key and tap Fit my voice. Set your voice range in Settings if you skipped it.

How do I record a cover?

Put on headphones, open a song, switch to Sing and tap Start singing. After the take, balance voice and band, then Save cover.

How do I loop a part?

In Practice, tap A at the start of the part and B at its end. Slow the tempo down if you need.

Why can't I open an Apple Music song?

Streamed and protected Apple Music songs can't be read by any app. Use songs you have as files.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Restore: Settings in the App → Restore purchases. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
