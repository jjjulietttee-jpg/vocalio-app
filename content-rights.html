Content Rights & Ownership Declaration

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · App: Acapo: Vocal Remover & Karaoke (formerly Vocalio) (bundle id com.lf.vocalio.app)

1. Acapo integrates no third-party music service

Acapo does not contain, embed, or connect to any third-party audio or video streaming service, music catalog, or discovery service. The app performs no search of any online music library, offers no browse or discovery interface for third-party content, and downloads no media from any third-party site, API, or public URL. There is no "paste a link" import, no YouTube or streaming-service integration, and no bundled or remote catalog of commercial recordings.

2. The only audio the app processes is audio the user already owns

Every import path in the app reads audio that is already present on the user's own device:

  • the user's own device music library (Apple's system MPMediaPicker);
  • files the user selects with the iOS Files picker;
  • audio the user records with the device microphone;
  • the audio track of videos in the user's own photo library;
  • our own bundled sample song (see section 3).

The audio a user selects is uploaded to our own processing backend solely to run AI vocal separation for that user, and the two resulting stems are returned to that same user's device. Nothing is published, shared, redistributed, or made available to any other user.

3. The bundled demo songs are public-domain compositions in our own new recordings

The demo songs in Acapo — House of the Rising Sun (traditional), Amazing Grace (John Newton, 1779), When the Saints Go Marching In (traditional), Scarborough Fair (traditional) and Oh! Susanna (Stephen Foster, 1848) — are compositions whose music and lyrics are in the public domain. The recordings shipped in the app, and the bundled sample song, were produced by us specifically for this app; they are not, and do not use, any commercial recording of these songs. The lyrics used are the traditional public-domain texts. All cover artwork was created by us.

We own all rights in these recordings and artwork. No commercial recording, no licensed master and no third-party artwork is included in the application binary.

4. History

An earlier build of this app (version 1.0, build 4) bundled short excerpts of commercial recordings as demo content and offered a "download from a link" import option. Both were removed in full before build 5. Version 1.1 replaced the earlier original demo set with the public-domain recordings above, and no trace of either remains in the current binary or in the App Store listing. The current build additionally states the ownership of the demo material on screen, in the app itself.

5. Declaration

We declare that Acapo holds all rights and permissions necessary for all audio, artwork, and functionality it contains, and that the app provides no access — authorized or otherwise — to any third-party audio or video streaming service, catalog, or discovery service.
